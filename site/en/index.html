The RGIE electrical file,
from diagram to board label.

Voltaplan draws the single-line diagram, the layout plan and the distribution board of a domestic installation, checks it against the RGIE (Belgian electrical installation regulations) and prints the file for the approved inspection body. Free, open source, in French, Dutch and English.

Open in the browser Install on a computer or phone Read the guide

Nothing to install for the web version: it runs in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and then works offline. Your projects stay on your computer (or in the storage service you choose); no Voltaplan server ever receives them.

Automatic single-line diagram

Drawn bottom to top like figure 3.1 of the RGIE, with the symbols of table 2.23 and A1, A2… numbering from the protective device.

Layout plan to scale

Walls, doors, windows, furniture; a photo or PDF background, scaled and straightened; walls traced automatically.

Drag-and-drop board

DIN rails, real device widths, 1:1 labels, comparison with a photo of the board.

RGIE check

Blocking non-conformities for a new installation, upgrade advice for an existing one, voltage drop and short-circuit.

Bill of materials

By characteristics and European standards, quantities and lengths, CSV export for your quote.

A4 / A3 file

Full title block, legend, measurements, labels, vector PDF. Marked “DRAFT” until the check passes.

Trikker

Import and export .trik files: carry on with your existing files.

Your files, wherever you want

This computer, the browser, GitHub, Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
