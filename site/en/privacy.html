Privacy policy

Updated : 24 November 2026

Voltaplan is a free and open source application (AGPL-3.0 licence) that runs on your device. There is no Voltaplan server, no account, no advertising and no analytics or telemetry. The website only delivers the application files.

What is stored, and where

  • Your projects: in the file you choose, in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on your device, or in your own online storage (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, GitHub) if you choose it.
  • Drafts, local versions, pending saves: in your device's browser, to recover your work after an interruption or a conflict.
  • Settings (language, layout, AI settings): in your device's browser. Your AI API key and GitHub token are encrypted with your passphrase, which is never stored.
  • Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox sign-in tokens stay in memory for the session only.

What is sent, and to whom

Nothing is sent without an action from you, and never to Voltaplan. The only possible transfers go directly from your device to the service you chose:

  • Artificial intelligence (optional, off by default): the board photo, reduced, to the provider you configure with your key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral) or to a local model on your computer. The recipient is shown before sending. Its terms apply and its cost is yours.
  • Google Drive: Google sign-in, then reading and writing only the files and folders created by Voltaplan or opened with it (drive.file permission). Voltaplan cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • OneDrive: only the app's own folder (Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder). Dropbox: only the app folder.
  • GitHub: the repository you specify, with your personal token.
  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages): the usual requests to download the application (IP address, browser), as for any website. No cookies or trackers are added by Voltaplan.

The application's content security policy blocks connections to any other service.

Use of Google user data

Data obtained through Google APIs (Voltaplan files in your Drive) is only used to open and save your projects, at your request. It is not shared with third parties, not used for any other purpose, and not stored anywhere other than your device and your Drive. Voltaplan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other processing

  • GitHub issues and discussions are public. Never attach client data.
  • Donations (GitHub Sponsors) are processed by GitHub under its own terms.

Your rights and responsibilities

Voltaplan holds no data about you: you can erase everything by clearing the site data in your browser and deleting your files. Revoke Voltaplan's access at any time from your Google, Microsoft or Dropbox account. You remain responsible for your clients' data (GDPR) when you store it in an online service.

Contact

Privacy question: issue on the repository; security vulnerability: private report.
