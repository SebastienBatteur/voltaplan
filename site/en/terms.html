What Voltaplan is

Free and open source software that helps draw the single-line diagram, layout plan and distribution board of an electrical installation, check part of the Belgian RGIE rules, and prepare the file for inspection by an approved body.

What Voltaplan is not

  • Not an inspection body: no certification, attestation or conformity report.
  • Not a guarantee of conformity: “no anomaly detected” only means no anomaly was found among the rules actually checked, with the information entered. The rules cover only part of the RGIE and are marked “to be reviewed” until a professional has checked them.
  • Not a protection study: calculations are conventional estimates.
  • Not a certified survey: a survey of an existing installation describes it as surveyed; values read from a photo or deduced must be confirmed on site.

Artificial intelligence

Optional and experimental. It uses the service you choose, at your cost and under its terms. Its readings are proposals: nothing is applied without your consent, and an unchecked value stays marked “unconfirmed”. An AI can be wrong.

Your responsibilities

  • Check the diagrams, values and conclusions before any professional use; you remain responsible for the installation and the documents you sign.
  • Protect your clients' data (see the privacy policy).
  • Keep your own backups: Voltaplan protects against accidental loss but does not store your projects.
